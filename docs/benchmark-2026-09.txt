backtracking solver beats the fast solver, by roughly 50x.

=== Notes on variance ===

Wall-clock totals on this laptop vary noticeably between runs (visible above,
e.g. top95 backtracking total ranges from 21.8s to 39.2s across three back to
back invocations), most likely from CPU turbo/thermal state and background
processes rather than the solvers themselves -- the node counts (the actual
algorithmic work) are identical every run. The speedup ratios are more stable
than the absolute numbers.
